How to choose the right salon shampoo for your hair

Key takeaways: choose your salon shampoo by hair need first and brand second. Color-treated hair needs a color-safe, sulfate-free formula, blonde and gray hair a toning shampoo once or twice a week, dry hair a hydrating shampoo, damaged hair a repair shampoo and fine hair a volumizing one.

Color-treated hair

Color fades faster with harsh cleansers and hot water, so look for a color-safe, sulfate-free shampoo and rinse with lukewarm water. Pureology Hydrate Shampoo is a favorite for dry, color-treated hair, while Redken Color Extend Magnetics and the Biolage ColorLast shampoo help keep color vibrant. After lightening, L'Oréal Professionnel Metal Detox targets metal deposits that make hair fragile.

Blonde, gray and silver hair

A toning shampoo deposits pigment that neutralizes brassiness. Purple shampoo cancels yellow tones in blonde, gray and silver hair, while blue shampoo targets orange tones in lightened brunette hair. Use your toning shampoo once or twice a week: Matrix So Silver is a salon classic.

Dry, frizzy or curly hair

Dry hair needs a hydrating shampoo that adds moisture without weighing it down. Redken All Soft, Matrix Food For Soft and Biolage Hydra Source are good examples of a moisture shampoo. For curls, Redken All Soft Mega Curls keeps the curl pattern defined, and an anti-frizz shampoo such as Redken Frizz Dismiss or Matrix Mega Sleek keeps hair smooth in humidity.

Damaged hair

Hair weakened by heat, bleach or chemical services needs a repair shampoo, such as Redken Extreme, Pureology Strength Cure or L'Oréal Professionnel Absolut Repair. On bleached hair, bond repair formulas from Olaplex help protect the hair structure.

Fine or thinning hair

Choose a lightweight volumizing shampoo and keep rich masks to the ends. Redken Volume Injection, Biolage Volume Boost and Matrix High Amplify add lift, and the Nioxin systems are designed for thinning hair.

Oily scalp, buildup and dandruff

A clarifying shampoo used every week or two removes the buildup left by styling products and hard water: try Redken Hair Cleansing Cream or Biolage Scalp Sync Clarifying. For flakes, choose an anti-dandruff formula like Biolage Scalp Sync Anti-Dandruff. Paul Mitchell Tea Tree Special gives a fresh, tingling cleanse.

Which shampoo do hairdressers recommend?

Most hairdressers recommend a professional shampoo matched to your hair type rather than a single brand. Redken, Pureology, Biolage, Matrix, L'Oréal Professionnel and Paul Mitchell are among the most requested brands at Beauty Plus Salon.

How often should I use purple shampoo?

Once or twice a week is enough for most blonde, gray or silver hair. Use a color-safe shampoo the rest of the time to avoid a lilac tint.
